## Fixes

### Black video posters, part two

Yesterday's backfill replaced the black posters on the server, but devices kept hiding or showing the old ones. Three separate problems:

- **Blank verdict never expired:** A device that saw a black poster stored a "blank" verdict with no expiry, so after the backfill phones kept hiding the repaired posters. A verdict now holds for 24 hours, then the poster is re-fetched past the year-long HTTP cache and probed again. Verdicts saved by the old build count as expired, so installed apps heal on their first launch of this build. (`1ce50d2`)
- **Stale cached poster:** In the app the old poster stayed in the WebView cache for a year, stored without CORS headers by the displaying `<img>`, so the CORS blank-check probe always failed and counted as fine. The probe now downloads the poster with `cache: reload`, judges the current bytes and overwrites the stale copy, and feed tiles and the chat gallery show the fresh copy at once. (`04bc843`)
- **No poster at all:** Videos with a missing or black poster fell back to a `#t=` first-frame hint, which desktop Chrome paints but the Android WebView does not, so they were empty tiles in the app. A frame is now drawn from the clip itself, the same capture as an upload, in feed tiles, chat bubbles and the chat gallery. On the owner's device it is also stored as the real `_thumb.webp`, and frames are kept per session and wiped on sign-out. (`1799484`)

### Media viewer close

Closing transitioned the overlay's background, a full-screen repaint every frame. The background now drops at once and the backdrop fades by opacity, with the media settling on the same curve. A centre tap with the controls hidden only shows them, and the next one plays or pauses. The play and pause flash no longer pops away mid-fade and has no per-frame backdrop blur. (`cfa7b54`)

### Toasts and bottom nav

On phones a toast always left room for the bottom nav, a large empty gap on screens without it. The nav now flags when it is on screen. Without it, toasts sit near the edge or just above the chat composer, which publishes its height. A toast travels with the nav on its own 0.26 s curve when scrolling hides or shows it, and the auth-page toast drops its phantom nav gap. (`13b0896`)

## Improvements

### Community descriptions

Descriptions are normalised the same way on the server (a DTO transform before validation) and in the app. Runs of spaces and tabs collapse, blank filler letters such as U+3164 collapse, zero-width spaces go, and ends are trimmed. Line breaks are kept, with at most one blank line. The API refuses more than 10 line breaks, and the forms fold extras into spaces. Every surface normalises on render, and a script fixes stored rows, run on development. (`747e1d4`)

### Community cards

- **Layout:** The card is rebuilt as a header row plus description: a smaller icon, the name over the member count, and Join on one line, with the description across the full width below in two lines. The member count matches the post handle size and the name is a step larger. Join is a 30 px pill with a 44 px touch target. Cards get a visible 1.5 px border, the Campus cards drop their shadow, and the skeleton matches. (`8facda4`)
- **Text and icon:** The Join pill was taller than the name and set the height of its line, so cards with a button had a wider gap between name and member count than cards without. The pill now overhangs the line evenly and the name sets its height. The icon aligns with the top of the text. (`8541dc8`)

### Search borders

Every bordered element on the search page (the search shell, filter and recent chips, result cards, load-more button and skeletons) uses the same 1.5 px border in the standard border colour as the community cards, because the light border colour all but vanished on white. The header search box, which other screens share, is unchanged. (`a0a8cdc`)

### Bottom nav

The tapped tab shows as active on the tap itself instead of waiting for the route, which lagged on history jumps and lazy pages and could flash the wrong tab when a jump was corrected. The icon swap, colour change and press dip are gone, so the switch happens in one frame. Messages shows a blue dot instead of a count, with the count kept in its label. Campus gets a real filled state drawn from its outline plus a tassel dot in both states, and the five icons are sized and stroked against each other so they read as one size and weight. (`4abce8c`)

### Campus background

The Campus page painted the document white on phones in light theme whether or not the user was verified. The verified hub now keeps the app's own page background, and the system bars follow it. The pick-a-college flow and the loading moment stay white, and dark mode stays black for both. (`6e9d3af`)
