## Fixes

### Audit findings

Three independent audit reports were checked against the code, and the findings that held up are applied. No migrations were needed. (`180e251`)

- **Messaging:** history, mark-as-read, reactions, delete-for-me and system messages now require an active participant, and suspended or deleted accounts can no longer send.
- **Posts and communities:** every single-post route and search enforces community access, list cursors no longer skip a row per page, block checks are added, and member lists and presence respect privacy.
- **Uploads:** support attachments are private with signed admin links, file type comes from the validated content type, and users cannot attach or upload into other people's or reserved media.
- **Auth:** `@VerifiedOnly` now works on HTTP, banned and deleted accounts are rejected, cookie users get per-user rate limits, reserved usernames are blocked at signup, and a Supabase outage no longer logs users out. Password reset revokes every session through a new recovery-token route.
- **Admin and limits:** force-logout and delete now revoke sessions, and new rate limits cover posting, commenting, community creation and presigned uploads.

### Dependencies

`npm audit` reported criticals in `proxy-addr` (IP spoofing through subnet matching) and `handlebars`, plus highs in `sharp`, `compression`, `undici`, `engine.io` and `brace-expansion`. They are patched with non-breaking updates, and overrides were added for `proxy-addr` and `undici`. (`1e97a4b`)

### Support email

Support attachments are private, so the links in the "request received" email returned 404 for everyone. The email now lists file names and sizes without a link. A signed URL was rejected because anyone the email is forwarded to could open the file until it expired. (`2c890b2`)

### Web and app

- **Home after going back:** going back rebuilt the virtualised feed from estimates, so rows measured above the restored position nudged the page. The feed now remembers each row's measured height, starts from the saved scroll position and restores it once, before paint. This also fixes the page snapping after a refresh. (`1679289`)
- **Community dialogs:** the mute dialog freezes the page behind it, leaving a community asks first in a matching dialog, the community menu button in the app moves into the header's right slot where the fixed header no longer paints over it, and instant notification cards are centred at every width. (`6d5d18f`)
- **Comments:** the @username, dot and time match the name's size and baseline. (`013c862`)
- **Stale chunks and caching:** protection against reloading stale chunks is preserved across startup, blanket immutable caching for asset responses is removed, and reload caching is validated and copes with blocked session storage. (`2c19f4a`, `61ef5ab`, `5b78a60`, `f36f15c`, `da6fd70`, `e47ab41`)

## Improvements

- **Verification prompt:** an unverified user who hits a create or send action now gets the "verify with a live selfie" dialog instead of a generic error. The API error carries a `VERIFICATION_REQUIRED` code, one shared handler opens the dialog, and the old plain-text 403 still works as a fallback. The reset-password page now makes a single call to the new finalize route, and admin tickets get links. (`8c9be4b`)
- **College picker:** on large screens the picker is left-aligned like the other list pages, and the college-email step stays centred in its 600 px column. (`558ffa8`)
- **Your communities:** the server now reads each community's newest post with one index probe each, instead of grouping over every post of every community the viewer is in. (`29c4620`)
