## Fixes

### Live connection in the app

Every token refresh revokes the old session row and creates a new one in the same login, and the minute session sweep closed any socket naming a revoked row. The app refreshes on resume and hourly, and a client never re-dials a socket the server closed, so live features such as confession reactions and Instant Match died until a restart. The sweep now keeps a socket while its login has a live session and adopts it, and the app re-dials once if its id changed. (`b84e9e8`)

### Confession reactions

Android's colour emoji are wider and taller than the 1.25 em slot each reaction in a confession's summary sat in, and both the slot and the summary clipped on every side, so the emoji were cut at the edges. The slot is now 1.4 em wide with room above and below, and clipping is sideways only, which is all the enter and leave animation needs. (`0571294`)

### Representative badge

The campus representative badge on a directory avatar went through several adjustments during the day:

- It loses its card-coloured ring and grows from 24 to 28 px. Pressing it no longer shrinks the whole card or shows a tap highlight, and a click on it no longer falls through to open the person's profile on a computer. (`81cc969`)
- The avatar is cut out round the badge with a single CSS mask, a 2.5 px notch where whatever is behind the avatar shows through, including the card in either theme. Only representatives' avatars are masked. (`96f04dc`)
- In the community member list the badge sits on the avatar's edge at 20 px, and its notch is halved to 1.25 px for a finer edge. (`b740286`, `70e3f30`)

### Online dot

The online dot was pinned to the avatar box's corner, so it hung off the circle, and it carried a drop shadow. It is now centred on the circle's edge at 45 degrees for every avatar size, about 15% larger and without a shadow, in one shared Avatar rule, so the messages list, the chat header and Home's Online Friends all change together. A follow-up makes the green part a touch smaller, about 10 px on a 48 px avatar instead of 11.5 px. (`4284d29`, `672292e`)

### Post composer text

What you type in the post composer, and its placeholder, were 0.95 rem at weight 400 with line height 1.5. The composer now uses the post card's type (1 rem, weight 450, line height 1.6), so a post reads the same before and after it is sent. Comment and chat inputs are unchanged. (`f10747d`)

## Improvements

### Members modal and community details

- The community member list has more room, a press and hover highlight, a 46 px avatar and a larger name that truncates before the Owner or Moderator pill does. Avatar and name open the profile by username, because matching on display name could open the wrong person. The modal's shadow, which was missing its `var()`, is fixed too. (`b740286`)
- The members modal fades in on desktop and slides up as a sheet on phones. Every way out (the X, a tap on the empty area, Back and Escape) plays the matching exit before it unmounts. A search field filters by name, @username or branch, and the sheet stays above the keyboard. (`c71bce0`)
- Community details labels (About, Interests, Community rules, Guidelines) are no longer forced into capitals and read a little larger. The gaps come from the layout alone (12 px on phones), and Guidelines are a quiet numbered list with hairlines instead of coloured icon tiles. (`35a9b1b`)

### Soft card shadow

Posts, the post composer, Campus community cards, Home's Your communities card, the profile card, the "No posts yet" card and the community hero carry the confession card's soft lift in light mode and stay flat in dark mode. The composer draws its shadow from its own layer, so it no longer vanishes and pops back while the composer opens or closes. (`67607b3`)
