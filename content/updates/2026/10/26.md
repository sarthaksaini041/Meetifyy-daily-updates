## Fixes

### Live connection died after a token refresh

Every token refresh revokes the old session row and makes a new one in the same login, and the minute session sweep closed any socket naming a revoked row. The app refreshes on resume and hourly, and a client never re-dials a socket the server closed, so its live features (confession reactions, Instant Match) died until a restart. The sweep now keeps a socket while its login has a live session and adopts it. The app also re-dials once if its session id changed. (`b84e9e8`)

### Clipped reaction emoji

Android's colour emoji are wider and taller than the 1.25em slot each emoji in a confession's reaction summary sat in, and the slot and summary both clipped on every side. The slot is now 1.4em wide with room above and below, and both clip sideways only, which is all the animation needs. (`0571294`)

### Directory badge

The representative badge on a directory avatar loses its ring and grows from 24 to 28 px. Pressing it no longer shrinks the whole card or shows a tap highlight, and on a computer a click on it no longer falls through and opens the profile. It then sits in a notch: a 2.5 px gap where the avatar is masked away with a single CSS mask on representatives' avatars only, so whatever is behind shows through in either theme instead of a ring painted in one fixed colour. (`81cc969`, `96f04dc`)

## Improvements

### Community members

- **Member list:** Rows get more room, a press and hover highlight, a 46 px avatar and a 1 rem name that truncates before the Owner or Moderator pill does. The representative badge moves onto the avatar's edge at 20 px in a transparent notch, then the notch is halved to 1.25 px. Avatar and name open the profile by username, because matching on display name could open the wrong person. The modal's shadow, which was missing its `var()`, is fixed. (`b740286`, `70e3f30`)
- **Members modal:** It fades in on desktop and slides up as a sheet on phones. Every way out (the X, a tap on the empty area, Back and Escape) plays the matching exit before it unmounts, and closes once however often it is asked. A search field filters by name, `@username` or branch, Escape clears it first, and the sheet stays above the keyboard. (`c71bce0`)
- **Details:** Section labels (About, Interests, Community rules, Guidelines) are sentence case and read a little larger in the main text colour. Guidelines are a quiet numbered list with hairlines instead of coloured icon tiles, and the gaps between the header, About and Guidelines are an even 12 px on phones. (`35a9b1b`)

### Online dot and shadows

- The online dot is centred on the avatar circle's edge at 45 degrees for every size, about 15% larger, with the white ring inside its size and no shadow, then a touch smaller (24% to 21% of the avatar). One shared Avatar rule changes the messages list, chat header and Home's Online Friends together. (`4284d29`, `672292e`)
- Posts, the post composer, Campus community cards, Home's Your communities card, the profile card and the community hero carry the confession card's soft lift in light mode and stay flat in dark mode. The composer's shadow comes from its own layer that moves with the expand clip, so it no longer vanishes and pops back. (`67607b3`)

### Composer type

The post composer's text and placeholder were 0.95rem at weight 400 and line height 1.5 from the shared mention editor, while a post card's body is 1rem, weight 450, line height 1.6. The composer now sets the card's type, so a post reads the same before and after it is sent. Comment and chat inputs are unchanged. (`f10747d`)
