## Fixes

### App sign-out revoked nothing on the server

The installed app has no cookie, so its sign-out previously revoked nothing server-side. `session/logout` now accepts the refresh token in the body from the native origin when there is no cookie, and revokes the whole login family so a just-rotated token cannot leave a live session. Login and `session/adopt` end the session the request already held. (`2692389`)

### Devices page

The device list no longer returns or shows IP addresses. Sessions from the installed app, detected from the request `Origin`, are labelled "Meetifyy app" and listed with `client: app|web`. (`2692389`)

### Cookie banner and cached chats

- The shared App tree mounted the website's cookie notice in the Capacitor build too, so every fresh install showed it. The app sets no cookies and runs no analytics, so the banner is web-only, and the information stays under Settings, Storage & Privacy.
- Sign-out and account switch never cleared the `meetifyy_messages` IndexedDB, leaving the previous account's cached conversations on disk. They are cleared now.
- The cookie policy text is corrected.

(`75cf6b4`)

### Scrolling, keyboard and splash in the app

- **Profile:** Scroll-driven animations stalled flicks on the APK, so header effects are written from the scroll event instead, with a sticky cover and avatar. The device nav bar clears with the app's bottom nav.
- **Community view:** Pull-to-refresh zooms and blurs the cover like Profile. The details sheet clears the status bar, slides from the right and closes on Back.
- **Keyboard:** The WebView follows the keyboard frame by frame while it opens, with no blank strip, and with fewer per-frame restyles.
- **Splash:** It follows the app's chosen theme from the first frame.
- The community description limit is 250 everywhere, from a shared constant and the API DTO, and the Home skeleton is redesigned with a cheaper media shimmer.

(`4010bde`)

### UI fixes

- **Sheet pages:** They filled `100dvh` under the status-bar padding, so every short settings screen scrolled by the inset. Settings fields get a thin outline in both themes, and the post view keeps its header with the back button. (`5cf4f51`)
- **Dark dropdown:** `CustomSelect` used tokens no theme defines, so their light fallbacks showed in dark mode, a white highlight under white text. (`cd4cf98`)
- **Sidebar logo:** A community logo in the sidebar was stretched and long names ran past the box. (`2fb1b40`)

## Improvements

### Help & Support

- **Hub:** Report a problem and Share an idea modals (a sheet on phones, a dialog on large screens) file to the support queue, signed in or as a guest. They support screenshots, automatic app version and device details, one submission per press, and keep input on network failure. "Your requests" lists tickets with a detail view showing what was sent and the team's replies. A safety centre lists 112 and national helplines. (`c174e05`)
- **API:** `SupportTicket.type` (`PROBLEM` or `IDEA`) is an additive migration, and ideas are filed at low priority. `POST /api/support/requests/mine` files as the signed-in account, because the public route treated an expired token as anonymous, which left tickets unlinked. `GET .../mine` and `/mine/:ticketNumber` are scoped to the session's account in the query, and internal notes are never returned. (`2b4b9d6`)
- **Diagnostics:** The attached details are in plain words behind a disclosure, and the Your requests tile shows only for signed-in users. (`cd4cf98`)

### Help articles

Twelve seeded articles described paths or features that do not exist: delete lives under Privacy & Security with a 30-day recovery, usernames and posts cannot be edited, campus events have no RSVP, notifications are push and email only, and so on. Corrections only touch seed rows no admin has edited. Five new articles cover verification, password and devices, visibility, first-year separation and Instant Match. (`e4c986e`)

### Press animation removed

`pressFeedback` scaled almost every tappable control on press, on the website and in the app. It is removed along with its install calls, tests and opt-outs, and controls that want press feedback style their own `:active` state. (`9a49ba0`)

### Emails

Emails no longer paint their own background. Page, card and panels are transparent or translucent tints, with a `prefers-color-scheme` stylesheet so text stays readable on dark clients, and the gutters are tighter. The Instagram link points at meetifyy.app. The Supabase auth templates are re-rendered for dev and prod. (`d97912b`)
