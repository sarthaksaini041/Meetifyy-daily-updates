## Fixes

### Devices and sign-out

- The device list no longer returns or shows IP addresses. Sessions from the installed app are labelled "Meetifyy app", and the panel shows a platform and recency line, a pending state per row and refreshes after a failed revoke.
- The app has no cookie, so its sign-out used to revoke nothing on the server. Logout now accepts the refresh token in the body from the app, and revokes the whole login family so a just-rotated token cannot leave a live session.
- Login and session adoption end the session the request already held, so signing in again no longer lists the same device twice.

(`2692389`)

### Privacy and cookies

- The installed app showed the website's cookie notice on every fresh install, even though it sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The banner is now web-only.
- Sign-out and account switch never cleared the cached conversations stored on the device, which left the previous account's messages on disk. They are cleared now.
- The Cookie and Privacy policies described a session token in local storage and no server cookies. They now describe the HttpOnly session cookies on the web, the Keystore in the app and Sentry error reports, and the consent version is bumped so the changed notice is shown once more.

(`75cf6b4`)

### Scrolling and layout

- **Settings and posts:** sheet pages filled the screen under the status-bar padding, so every short settings screen scrolled by the inset. Mobile settings no longer reserve padding for a bottom nav they lack, and the post view keeps its header with the back button instead of auto-hiding it. (`5cf4f51`)
- **Profile and community:** scroll-driven animations stalled flicks on the APK, so header effects are written from the scroll event, with a sticky cover and avatar. Community view gets pull-to-refresh that zooms and blurs the cover like Profile, and its details sheet clears the status bar, slides from the right and closes on Back. The community description limit is 250 everywhere. The WebView follows the keyboard frame by frame while it opens, and the splash follows the app's chosen theme from the first frame. (`4010bde`)
- **Community sidebar:** the logo no longer stretches, and long names stay inside the box. (`2fb1b40`)

### Help dropdown and modals

The dropdown used theme colours that no theme defines, so its light fallbacks showed in dark mode (a white highlight under white text). Attached details in the report and idea modals are in plain words behind a disclosure, the Your requests tile shows only for signed-in users, and the help footer has a mail pill and dot-separated links. (`cd4cf98`)

## Improvements

### Help & Support hub

- Report a problem and Share an idea open as a sheet on phones and a dialog on large screens, and file to the support queue signed in or as a guest. They support screenshots, automatic app version and device details, one submission per press and keep your input on a network failure. (`c174e05`)
- Your requests lists what you filed, with a detail view showing what was sent and the team's replies. The Safety centre has 112 and national helplines, and Settings has grouped common questions that link to their settings. The installed app opens the Help page on the website instead of bundling it. (`c174e05`)
- On the API, a support request is either a problem or an idea (ideas are filed at low priority), and requests from a signed-in user are linked to their account. Previously an expired token was treated as anonymous, which left such tickets unlinked and missing from Your requests. Listing is scoped to the session's account, and internal notes are never returned. (`2b4b9d6`)

### Emails

Email templates no longer paint their own background: the page, card and panels are transparent or translucent, with a dark-scheme stylesheet so text stays readable on dark clients, and gutters are tighter. The Instagram link points at meetifyy.app. (`d97912b`)

### Press animation removed

The app-wide press animation, which scaled almost every tappable control, is removed. Controls that want press feedback style their own pressed state. (`9a49ba0`)

### Home skeleton

The Home skeleton has no composer placeholder and simpler post placeholders, and the media shimmer is cheaper. (`4010bde`)

## Documentation

- Twelve seeded help articles described paths or features that do not exist (for example, delete lives under Privacy & Security with a 30-day recovery, usernames and posts cannot be edited, and campus events have no RSVP). They are corrected, but only rows no admin has edited, and five new articles cover verification, password and devices, visibility, first-year separation and Instant Match. (`e4c986e`)
