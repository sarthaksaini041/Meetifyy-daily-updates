## Improvements

### Personalised ranking, with no new infrastructure

Everything is SQL scoring per request or cached aggregates. There is no ML service.

- **Home feed:** A ranked For You feed scores freshness times (relationship + interest + capped engagement), pins fresh posts from follows, allows at most two per author in any ten, and goes chronological past the 7-day window. Pages come from an order cached per session in Redis. `FEATURE_FEED_RANKING=false` restores the chronological feed with no app update.
- **Affinity:** A `UserAffinity` table is rebuilt every 6 hours by a BullMQ job from likes, comments, bookmarks and DMs, and feeds the close-friends signal.
- **People suggestions:** Friends-of-friends and follows-you pools are scored on mutuals, branch, batch, communities and interests, with a weighted draw and a reason line such as "Followed by Rahul + 2 more".
- **Community discovery:** Ranked by friends inside, interest match, campus presence and recent activity. Dormant giants sink, and pending requests are excluded.
- **Search:** Ranked in SQL by match tier, then closeness. This fixes exact matches missing behind newer partial ones, suspended accounts in results, `LIKE` wildcards and other campuses' campus-only communities.
- **Crew For You:** Social and interest candidate pools, capacity counted as the join does, own and full activities excluded, word-start interest matching, and answered activities sorted last.
- **Pickers:** Share and @mention pickers show your own people first, and exact and starts-with matches are never crowded out.
- **Notifications:** Grouped per post, comment and activity, and for follows.

### Hashtags and Trending

Hashtags were never saved. Posts now index them on create, and a migration backfills existing posts. Hashtags are tappable in posts and comments, `#tag` search is exact, and a "Trending on campus" panel shows on Home and Search. A tag needs at least two people and public posts only to trend.

(`940cf3d`)

### First-year isolation removed

First-year isolation is removed at every layer. `batchYear` is kept and still derived at sign-in, and installed apps still receive `isFirstYearStudent` and `messagingRestricted` as false so older builds keep working. A migration archives the help article that described the feature. (`940cf3d`)

## Fixes

The installed app no longer shows a Cookie Policy link in the settings footer or the cookie preferences modal, since the app sets no cookies. Both point at the Privacy Policy there, and the website is unchanged. (`337bdd5`)
