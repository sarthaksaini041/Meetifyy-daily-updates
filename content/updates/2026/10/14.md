## Improvements

### Personalised ranking at no extra cost

Everything is SQL scoring per request or cached aggregates, with no ML service and no new infrastructure.

- **Home feed:** a ranked For You feed scored as freshness × (relationship + interest + capped engagement). Fresh posts from people you follow are pinned, at most two per author appear in any ten, and the feed turns chronological past the 7-day window. It is paged over an order cached per session in Redis, and `FEATURE_FEED_RANKING=false` restores the chronological feed with no app update.
- **Close friends:** a table of user affinity, rebuilt every 6 hours by a queue job from likes, comments, bookmarks and DMs, feeds the close-friends signal.
- **People suggestions:** friends-of-friends and follows-you pools, scored on mutuals, branch, batch, communities and interests, with a reason line on the card ("Followed by Rahul + 2 more").
- **Community discovery:** friends inside, interest match, campus presence and recent activity. Dormant giants sink and pending requests are excluded.
- **Crew For You:** social and interest candidate pools, with capacity counted as the join does, own and full activities excluded, word-start interest matching and answered activities sorted last.
- **Pickers:** share and @mention pickers show your own people first, and exact and starts-with matches are never crowded out.
- **Notifications:** grouped per post, comment, activity and for follows.

### Hashtags and Trending on campus

Hashtags were never saved. Posts now index them on create, a migration backfills existing posts, hashtags are tappable in posts and comments, and `#tag` search is exact. A Trending on campus panel (rising tags with at least two people, public posts only) shows on Home and Search.

### Search ranking

Search ranks in SQL by match tier, then closeness. This fixes exact matches hiding behind newer partial ones, suspended accounts appearing in results, LIKE wildcards matching, and other campuses' campus-only communities showing up.

### First-year isolation removed

First-year isolation is removed at every layer. The batch year is kept and still derived at sign-in, installed apps still receive `isFirstYearStudent` and `messagingRestricted` as false, and a migration archives the help article that described it. (`940cf3d`)

## Fixes

- The installed app sets no cookies, so the settings footer and the cookie preferences modal now point at the Privacy Policy instead of a Cookie Policy. The website is unchanged. (`337bdd5`)
