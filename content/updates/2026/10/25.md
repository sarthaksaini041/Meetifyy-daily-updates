## Improvements

### Confessions

- **Reactions:** confessions get five reactions (Love, Funny, Sad, Surprised, Relatable) in place of Relate. Existing relates become Relatable, and the old routes keep working for installed apps. Each change is one SQL statement, and the client coalesces rapid taps into one request per burst. Once a confession has reactions the card shows a summary that opens a counts modal, and confessions can be shared into chats. (`a366b04`, `a3c00ca`)
- **Audit:** Supabase's Data API is locked out of every table (row-level security on, anon and authenticated grants revoked). The public anon key could previously read and write all of them, including the account-to-handle map. Confessions requests no longer log the account, IP, device or body, posting is serialised per profile, and the minimum is now 15 characters. Onboarding appears only on the server's word that there is no name, and names get pixel avatars and a privacy hint. (`14b8805`)

### Native page transitions

The app animates opening and closing pages natively. It screenshots the window, draws it in an overlay and moves it with one animator, so the WebView itself never moves or re-rasters. Two effects share that engine: a slide, and an X-style centre opening where the page being left grows and dissolves on open and shrinks on close. History remembers the effect so Back replays it. (`ec9d848`) Posts, profiles, communities, Campus people and activities, the directory, Crew's Create activity and everything opened from search use the centre effect, and a post's four action buttons dissolve before the rest. (`39abe54`)

### Email change security

- Changing the sign-in email, changing the college email and removing it now need a fresh single-use reauth grant: the password, or a code to the current address for accounts without one. Each email may change once every 30 days, enforced under a row lock against the database clock. Removal is never blocked but restarts the college cooldown. A trigger on `auth.users` refuses Supabase's own self-service email change, which closes a direct bypass. Interrupted changes are reconciled from Supabase, and the notice to the old address is retried. (`dbb4252`)
- Settings and Campus ask for the password or an emailed code in a shared dialog before any email change or removal. (`82ee798`)

### Media and videos

- Messages take up to 15 MB a video and 5 MB an image, posts 25 MB a video and 5 MB an image, and voice notes 15 MB, each file on its own. The presigned upload signs the declared size, and confirm re-measures from storage. A post video can no longer ride into a chat. (`c26c326`)
- The video viewer's controls auto-hide, side taps reveal them on touchscreens, double-tap seeking chains, and fullscreen works in the app. Chat videos show their poster frame and real shape from the moment they are sent. Voice notes stop at 5 minutes with a countdown pill, and a recording is kept through failed uploads until sent or deleted. (`d324439`)

### Chat and campus interface

- Message rows are spaced from one place, same-author messages close up and bubbles are rounder and wider on phones. (`63b162e`, `b8e2f5d`)
- The directory API returns a campus representative flag, shown as a badge beside the name. (`611aaba`)

## Fixes

- **Status bars:** closing the media viewer left the page inert, so both bars were painted blue. The bars also re-sample during a scroll. Campus draws its red banner under a transparent status bar, and the whole banner, including the shortcut pills, is now sticky on phones. (`6a44748`, `95e5861`)
- **Reply bar:** it no longer covers the latest messages, because the composer reserves its height. The label reads "Replying to Aarav". (`8e5e9bf`)
- **Search and spacing:** on phones the search page is pure black in dark mode with 8 px between every result, and the profile card and its posts are exactly 8 px apart at every width. (`14c0303`, `ed3c183`)
- **Contacts:** every place that published a Gmail address or `hello@meetifyy.app` now uses `support@meetifyy.app` or `contact@meetifyy.app`, with silent new versions of the affected legal documents. (`d030f2d`) The About page still showed the old address because an environment variable overrode the new default, so both addresses are now constants. (`f67eafa`)

## Documentation
