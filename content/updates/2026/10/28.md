## Fixes

### Retried writes could be applied twice

The connection-retry middleware retried every transient connection failure, including a connection that broke while a write was on it, where the database may already have run the statement. Failures are now split in two: a connection that was never made always retries, and one lost mid-query retries only reads (`findMany`, `count` and the like). Nothing retries inside a transaction, and raw queries count as writes. (`941dc26`)

### Messages in landscape

The landscape adapter rewrote `@media` queries so a short, wide screen takes the phone layout, but not `@container` ones. Messages is a query container, and on a phone in landscape it is about 800 px wide, so its container rules chose the two-pane desktop branch while the media rules had already stacked the layout as a column. The chat landed below the screen and the right half stayed empty. The adapter now mirrors `@container messages` rules into the matching device-height `@media` block, or gates them to tall screens, so both agree. (`1be87f9`)

### Pickers and error screen

- The selection ring around a picked chat was always a circle, which cut across the corners of a group's rounded-square avatar. The Share, Invite Friends and group invite sheets share one stylesheet, so the avatar wrapper takes a group modifier with the avatar's own radius (24% of its size). (`568ddc9`)
- On the landing page, About, the legal documents and Help, a crash showed the contained error card floating on the pale blue gradient. The error screen fills the viewport on a flat surface (white, black in dark mode) with no card, border or shadow, and the gradient variables are flattened while it is up, so the scrollbar gutter and system bars are plain too. Only those routes use it, and in-app screens keep the contained card. (`6143a2b`)

## Improvements

### Group chats

- **Messaging:** Removed members' list previews stop at the moment they left, hidden chats come back and rejoin their live rooms, reads clear on every device, unsend has a time window, removals send a push, and the invite sheet uses the Share sheet.
- **Groups:** Settings are rewritten (mute, who can join, who can edit, transfer ownership), join requests reach admins live with a push, and closed groups refuse joins. Former members keep the name and photo from when they left, through an additive migration.
- **Mentions:** Group-only, for members and `@everyone`, picked locally and notified only to unmuted members. Muting now silences every alert and push.
- **Unread badge:** The navigation badge adds a server unread summary for chats beyond the 50 the inbox loads.
- **App:** A lost WebView renderer reopens the page it was on, the warm-start splash no longer leaves a black screen, chat and invite links are in the link filter, and every keyboard sheet slides with the keyboard.
- **Cleanup:** The composer's close draws its clip and the slide below it in one frame loop, so the next card never runs into it. About 40 unused files and exports are removed.

(`df8e311`)
