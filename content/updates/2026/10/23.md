## Improvements

### Anonymous Campus Confessions

Confessions live at `/campus/confessions`: a three-slide intro, a one-time anonymous name, and a campus feed with a composer, relates, delete and report.

- The API takes the campus from the caller's verified affiliation and returns only the handle as identity.
- Names, posts and relates are rate-limited, and duplicates within 24 hours are refused.
- Reports use the existing pipeline with a new `CONFESSION` target, scoped to the campus. Five open reports hide a confession, and moderators restore, hide or remove it through `PATCH /admin/confessions/:id`.
- Account deletion removes the user's confession profile, confessions and relates, and corrects the relate counts it leaves behind.
- The migration is additive: three tables, one enum and one `ReportTargetType` value. It had not yet been checked to build from an empty schema at this point.

(`4927d00`)

### Offline Campus, communities and activities

The app keeps an offline copy of Campus (its four lists, scoped to the college they were saved under) and of the last eight communities and activities opened. Offline, a community or activity with nothing saved shows a centred "You're offline" screen with a way back instead of a skeleton or "not found", and network-only actions are guarded. Pull-to-refresh offline shows the toast and keeps the screen as it is. (`2067fbc`)

### Opening screen

The installed app's front door is three slides you swipe, or jump to with the dots: people, events and plans, each with its own headline, photo fan and tiles. Swiping follows the finger: one registered CSS number drives the track, the photo parallax and three background circles that drift and resize between slides. The logo and wordmark are gone because the native splash has just shown them, and the name stays in a screen-reader-only heading. Light and dark are separate token sets, layouts were measured from 320 x 568 to 412 x 915 plus a tablet and sideways, and motion is transform and opacity only and respects reduced motion. Tests cover swiping, flicks, the edges, dots, both doors and what is hidden from assistive tech. (`7af6045`)

## Fixes

- **Offline icons:** Community and group icons loaded through the API's redirect, which expired after an hour, so offline icons fell back to the initial. They now load straight from the public media host, which caches them for a year, like people's avatars. `CommunityAvatar` falls back from the direct thumbnail to the direct original, then the API. The offline toast just says "You're offline". (`50083f5`)
- **Image flash:** Every image started hidden and faded in behind a skeleton, even when the WebView already had it cached, because the app's "already loaded" sets are empty after each launch. An image that is complete at mount or loads within 100 ms now shows at once. Post tiles use the known thumbnail URL from the first frame, and the own Profile tab is warmed from the device copy at idle. (`2444d0c`)
