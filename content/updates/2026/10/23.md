## Improvements

### Confessions

Anonymous campus Confessions open at `/campus/confessions`, with a three-slide intro, a one-time anonymous name and a campus feed with a composer, relates, delete and report. The API takes the campus from the caller's affiliation, returns only the handle as identity, and rate-limits names, posts and relates. Duplicates within 24 hours are refused. Reports use the existing pipeline with a new confession target, five open reports hide a confession, and moderators can restore, hide or remove it. Account deletion removes the user's confession profile, confessions and relates and corrects the counts. (`4927d00`)

### Offline

- The app keeps an offline copy of Campus (its four lists, scoped to the college they were saved under) and of the last eight communities and activities opened. Offline, one with nothing saved shows a centred "You're offline" screen with a way back instead of a skeleton or "not found". (`2067fbc`)
- Community and group icons load straight from the public media host, which caches them for a year, so they stay visible offline instead of falling back to the initial after the redirect expired. The offline toast is shorter. (`50083f5`)

### Opening screen

The installed app's front door is three slides you can swipe or jump to with the dots: people, events and plans, each with its own headline, photo fan and tiles. Swiping follows the finger, with the photo parallax and three background circles drifting between slides. (`7af6045`)

## Fixes

Images no longer flash when a screen is opened again. Every image started hidden and faded in behind a skeleton even when the WebView already had it cached. Now an image that is complete at mount, or loads within 100 ms, shows at once. (`2444d0c`)
