## Fixes

### Site unscrollable after launch

The launch shell locked scrolling with inline styles on `html` and `body`. Pages and overlays that save an inline `overflow` to restore later saved that lock as the original value and wrote it back after the shell lifted, so the site stayed unscrollable until a reload. The lock is now a class on `html`. The landing and not-found pages clear their overrides on unmount instead of restoring a captured value, and the legal update gate uses the shared scroll lock. (`8fcf5cf`)

### Layout and visual fixes

- **Messages:** removed the square background behind the rounded box on desktop.
- **Saved:** larger title, and a boxed card on large screens like Communities.
- **Notifications:** clearer section labels with dividers.
- **Profile:** the academic catalogue is read from cache before the skeleton lifts, so tag rows no longer re-balance late.
- **Media viewer:** the image no longer jumps back to the centre before the fade when closing.
- **Chat (app):** the composer clears the navigation bar, and the details header clears the status bar.

(`7767b51`)

### Keyboard follow-ups

The keyboard work below was reviewed and corrected:

- The new text-entry behaviour is installed-app only, so the website keeps its URL bar collapse, pinch zoom and single-tap keyboard on iOS.
- A closing keyboard's final resize can no longer leave text-entry mode stuck on.
- The viewport baseline resets when no keyboard can be the cause (split-screen, exited fullscreen video), and pinch zoom is not mistaken for a keyboard.
- When text entry ends, system-bar sampling and inline video pick up what they skipped.
- The composer's keyboard-hidden collapse only applies when the text field has focus, and the refocus keeps the platform caret.
- Date, time, read-only and disabled inputs are not treated as text fields.

(`88da85c`)

## Improvements

### Steadier composer and keyboard

Opening the keyboard made the feed jump. The causes found on the device and their fixes:

- Post media were sized in `vh`, which shrinks with the keyboard, so every image resized. They now use a keyboard-free viewport unit.
- The bottom nav and Instant Match button rode the shrunken viewport onto the keyboard. In the app they now stay on the physical bottom edge.
- The virtualized feed no longer mounts and unmounts rows as the keyboard opens.
- The system-bar colour sampler no longer forces a style recalculation on every change (about 40 ms per tap), and inline video no longer pauses when the viewport shrinks.
- The composer expands with a smooth transition, then raises the keyboard. Android Back now collapses an empty composer, and focus never scrolls the page.

(`bf3cf6e`)

### Legal pages are static

The four legal documents are static content copied verbatim from the published production versions, rendered without an API call. The Legal section is removed from the admin panel and the backend admin API. Consent tracking and the acknowledgement gate are unchanged. In the installed app, legal routes open the same path on the website in the system browser. Help & Support in the app gets an app top bar instead of the website navbar and footer, and follows the dark theme. (`37f61b7`)

### Instagram on the website share sheet

The website share sheet has an Instagram tile that copies the link, since Instagram has no web share URL. The app keeps its Story tile. (`d7879fc`)
