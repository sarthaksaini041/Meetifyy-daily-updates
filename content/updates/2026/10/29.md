## Fixes

### Polls

Poll options were shown in the order of their random ids. A new `position` column keeps the order they were written in, and every read orders by it. Voting is by option id and idempotent, so a repeat answers with the vote that stands and a race onto the unique constraint no longer returns a 500. The live vote update carried the voter's own choice, so every other viewer saw the poll as voted by them. It now carries counts only, and clients merge only counts. Vote counts also stopped drifting: purging an account lowers them, a migration recounts them once, and duplicate options are refused. (`b5a5bab`)

### Removed members saw nothing

The line about a departure was written just after the member's `leftAt`, and their history ends at `leftAt`, so a removed member lost it the moment they reloaded. It is now written at `leftAt` and carries what happened (`member_removed` or `member_left`). The person it is about reads "@admin removed you from the group" or "You left the group", in the chat and in their chat list, and after a reload the composer still tells the two apart from that line. The two controllers' copies of the broadcast are one helper, and older apps keep the stored text. (`b3608db`)

### Sign-in lost the link you came through

A signed-out visitor who opens a deep link is sent to sign in, and the destination is kept. The redirect read and deleted it while rendering, so a second render before the redirect committed found nothing and went Home. It now only reads it and deletes it once the redirect has committed. Signing up never used it at all, because the last signup step went straight to Home, and it now goes to the kept destination first. (`527d21f`)

### Settings

Saving Profile, Academic, Interests or Password used to close the panel and jump to `/settings`. It now stays on the panel, waits for the server, says "Saving..." and shows the saved values. Switches (private profile, online status, read receipts, email notifications) save as they are flipped and flip back with an error if the save fails. Back follows where you came from, forms reload the stored values when opened, and leaving one with unsaved edits asks "Discard changes?". (`e8e7351`)

### Headers in the app

- **Empty covers:** With no cover picture the pinned Profile and Community header was grey while its title, buttons and status-bar icons were styled for a photo, white on grey, so they disappeared. A cover with no picture now gets the theme's text colour and a faint neutral chip behind the buttons. (`86ecb6f`) A community's no-cover tint was see-through, so its own name and icon showed beneath the header's title. It sits on a solid base now. (`c4fcdac`)
- **Post page:** The top bar runs under a clear status bar and pads by the status-bar inset, with the page's own surface behind it, so comments scrolling beneath never show through the icons. It reaches the installed app with the next APK. (`c38a301`)
- **Campus:** Confessions, Directory, Events and Communities keep their banner pinned like the hub does, and the Campus page sits on plain white or black on phones. The unverified screen carried its bottom padding twice because a same-named page class hid the shared rule. (`d68936b`, `1ff3e86`)

### Smaller fixes

- **Comments:** Every row ran its fade-in each time the section mounted, so coming back from a profile made the whole list look freshly loaded. A row now decides once, when it mounts, whether to ease in. (`5a3d517`)
- **Community logo:** The generated default letter sat off-centre depending on the font. The glyph is measured and its ink bounds are centred, and existing logos keep their stored picture. (`029efbb`)
- **Add your campus dialog:** On large screens the header had no room under the missing drag handle, the cap icon loses its drop shadow, and the back arrow moves to the panel's top-left from 769 px. (`75393f2`)

## Improvements

### Group invites checked by the server

- **Invites:** Joining a group you are not in now names the invite you came through: a link code (a new `GroupInviteLink`, valid 7 days, which admins can reset) or the chat card's message id. A card counts only for someone in the chat it was sent in, while its sender is still in the group, for 48 hours. Expired, revoked or missing invites cannot join or request, whatever "Who can join" says.
- **Preview:** The preview returns that answer as `joinState` (`INVITE_EXPIRED`, `REQUESTED`, `REJECTED` with `retryAt` and so on) and up to five members, only for an invite really given to the viewer.
- **Requests:** They stay as rows (`PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`), and approve and decline move only a pending one inside a transaction, so two admins cannot both apply it. A declined person may ask again after 24 hours, and pending requests no longer lapse. The admins' "has requested to join" lines come from a reviewer-only endpoint, never a message, and the requester gets a live decision event and an approval push.
- **Safety:** The migration is additive and was built from empty with every earlier one. A new `msg.invitelink.user` rate limit applies, and tests cover each invite state, the races on a real Postgres and the admin-only boundary.

(`4f6a87a`)

### One group preview

A group invite card in a chat always says "View group", an expired one too, and opens a sheet with the group's picture, name, up to five members, the member count and one button. The button is the server's answer: Join group, Request to join, Request pending, Request declined, Invite link expired or Open group. Invite links are `meetifyy.app/g/<code>`, handled by a web route, both `vercel.json` files, the App Links filter and push and deep-link intents, and open the same sheet over Messages after sign-in if needed. Admins get Reset invite links in Group settings, and owners and admins see "has requested to join the group" in the timeline with a Review button that opens Join requests, now with Approve and Reject. (`62b26cf`)

### Activities: "I'm out"

An activity's page offers "I'm out" next to "I'm in", and an invitee can decline an invitation from the page itself. "I'm out" is stored as a `DECLINED` member row, with no migration, and declines any live invitation. A declined invitation still lets the invitee see the activity so they can change their mind, but never surfaces it in discovery. The Attendees card lists Going (host first) and Not going. Every member count, the capacity check and search count only `MEMBER` rows. (`313b16e`)

### Campus verification

Someone without a verified college sees a "Find your campus" picture, a glass search field and the colleges as square cards with their real logos. Choosing a college only opens its verification screen, sliding in from the right on a phone, with a tall email field whose placeholder uses the college's real domain. Nothing is sent until an address is submitted, and the server still checks domain and uniqueness. The "Add your campus" request sheet is slimmer, with a hand-built 3D graduation-cap icon, and drags down to close on a phone. `GET /api/academics/colleges` lists each college's primary email domain first, so a college with several domains no longer shows a secondary one in the hint. (`491a28d`, `e1c2fcb`, `7c20472`)

### Communities tabs

Yours and Explore are two panes side by side. Choosing a tab slides to its pane, and a sideways swipe drags them with the finger (touch only, a quarter of the width or a flick switches). The indicator is a 44 px rounded bar centred under the active tab and moving with the swipe. At rest only the active pane is on the page. (`6d4cecb`)

### Instant Match requests

A request to someone who is also searching waited 30, 60 or 90 seconds depending on the activity, plus a 5 second grace, and was only noticed by the 10 second sweep. It now waits exactly 30 seconds and is withdrawn by a delayed job at that moment, with the sweep as backstop. The sender is told it was withdrawn and the other person that it expired. (`3a207e8`)

### Header and icon consistency

The Profile and Community header is 64 px below the status bar instead of 56, and the text shadow on the title is removed. All 26 `ArrowLeft` usages and 14 hand-copied inline SVGs now render one shared chevron, and the unused export is removed so the shaft arrow cannot come back. (`ebf45f2`, `e456600`)

## Testing

Two `CampusEventForm` tests picked a day three days ahead from a calendar that opens on the current month. In the last days of a month that day is on the next month, so the tests failed, and with them the frontend CI step, until the month rolled over. The helper now steps the calendar forward until the day is shown. (`478a437`)
