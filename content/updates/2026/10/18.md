## Improvements

### Open signup and gender

Signup accepts any email, with no college step. The login address no longer links a college. The signup email is the primary email and never grants an affiliation, even on a college domain. Gender is a new nullable `User.gender` enum, required by the new signup UI but optional in the API so older APKs that do not send it keep working. (`26c4e7f`, `9be7522`, `393c1bb`)

### Campus by verified affiliation

- **Model:** A new `CollegeAffiliation` is proven by an OTP sent to the college email, or backfilled for legacy accounts. `User.collegeId` and `User.collegeEmail` remain as a mirror of the active affiliation, so existing `collegeId` queries keep working. Only the affiliation service writes them. (`393c1bb`, `35fa909`)
- **Exclusive ownership:** The normalized address is unique across all rows, including revoked ones, until released. It is checked before the OTP is sent and again inside the verify transaction, with the unique index as the final arbiter. The error never identifies the owner. OTPs are bound to the address and college they were sent for, so a code cannot be redirected. (`393c1bb`, `35fa909`)
- **Enforcement:** Campus access is authorised by verified affiliation in communities, posts, activities, search, ranking and realtime. Ineligible requests get a 403 with `campus_affiliation_required`, or an empty list where the endpoint already returned one. (`189d94f`)
- **UI:** A Campus college picker, college-email verification and college requests. The redundant "College verified" badge is dropped from the Campus header. (`67dc3db`, `576f1b8`)
- **Legacy users:** Every existing account signed up with a confirmed email on an approved college domain, so a migration backfills one affiliation per user where the domain maps to the same college and the address is not claimed twice. Duplicates are skipped and reported by a dry-run script, which must show zero unconfirmed rows before the production deploy. (`393c1bb`, `296f944`)

### Personal sign-in email

Existing users whose primary email is their college email can add a personal email in Settings. An OTP goes to the new address, the current password is required, and the server updates the auth email and `User.email`. The old address gets a notice. A profile completion prompt asks for a personal email and gender, and "Later" snoozes it for 10 days or until the next sign-in, with a Settings dot for what is left. The options show one state at a time. (`0b58e1b`, `90a57a6`, `9ebff53`, `d65de10`, `138c882`, `b25e544`)

### Live-selfie gate narrowed

The selfie gate stays only on messaging, creating posts, communities and activities, and Instant Match, and now also on group joins. It is removed from likes, comments, bookmarks, votes, follows, joining communities, campus reads and similar actions. Android gets the camera permission for the selfie. (`07910a8`, `a46ee7b`)

### Legal documents

- **Admin-managed legal documents:** Admin-managed documents with a public version history and a consent cache shared across instances. This reverses the static legal pages added on 4 Oct. The pages load from the API with version history. (`e6bc70c`, `81126ef`, `4969c59`)
- **Update screen:** The legal update screen is redesigned, then simplified: centred, no document cards or small print, and actions hidden while reading. (`81126ef`, `63f7193`)
- Help articles and the Terms and Privacy text are updated for open signup and Campus verification. (`59d9d71`)

### Admin

The admin panel gets a Legal section, college logos and domains, and user affiliation with revoke. Campus reps are derived from affiliation. Logos are cropped and compressed in the browser, and the server enforces square logos and re-encodes them. (`853e29a`, `4969c59`, `b4973a9`)

### UI polish

- **Edit cover sheet:** It expands to full screen, with Upload beside Save and larger swatches, then opens at its usual height again with Upload and Save splitting the footer. (`79e731f`, `e833acc`)
- **Scrollbars and fields:** Scrollbars are hidden in the cover sheet, the Notifications box and the followers list. Settings fields, menus and notices are neutral in dark mode. (`8ce9a72`, `4ddbd09`, `c1c296b`)
- **Profile and Campus:** Profile tags share one row when they fit and the college gets a school emoji. Campus communities have a minimal empty state, and people tiles press smoothly. (`144f901`, `856170b`, `26c9e9b`)
- **Devices:** App sessions get a smartphone icon and this device a status dot. (`5a53e9a`)
- **Drawer and Instant Match:** The mobile drawer slides on a decelerate curve with its prefetches moved after the slide, and Instant Match chat and sheet ride the keyboard like Messages. (`853e4e9`, `54a46f0`)

## Fixes

The duplicate Blocked Contacts box is removed from visibility settings. (`c39dfeb`)
