## Improvements

### Open signup and Campus verification

Signup no longer requires a college email. Anyone can sign up with any email, and the college step and the college linking from the login address are gone. Signup now asks for gender. (`26c4e7f`, `9be7522`)

Belonging to a college is now a separate, verified step called Campus:

- **Verification:** the Campus college picker lets a user choose their college and verify with a college email, with exclusive ownership of that address, and request a college that is not listed. (`35fa909`, `67dc3db`)
- **Database:** college affiliation, gender and bound one-time codes are added to the schema, with a legacy backfill and a dry-run report. (`393c1bb`)
- **Authorisation:** Campus access in communities, posts, activities, search, ranking and realtime is now decided by a verified affiliation. (`189d94f`)
- **Rollout:** a migration and rollback plan covers signup and Campus verification. (`296f944`)

### Personal sign-in email

The sign-in email can be changed with a one-time code sent to confirm it, and the old address gets a notice. Settings has an Email panel on the shared settings layout, and existing users can complete their profile. The profile completion step has step dots, Later beside Save and full-box selection. (`0b58e1b`, `90a57a6`, `b25e544`, `138c882`)

### Live-selfie gate

The live-selfie check is limited to messaging, creating and Instant Match, and group joins are now gated as well. The camera permission for the selfie is added on Android. (`07910a8`, `a46ee7b`)

### Legal documents

Legal documents are admin-managed again, with a public version history and a consent cache shared across instances. The pages load from the API with version history, and the update screen is redesigned: centred, without document cards or small print, and with actions hidden while reading. (`e6bc70c`, `81126ef`, `63f7193`)

### Admin

The admin panel has a Legal section, college logos and domains, and user affiliation with a revoke action. On the API side, college logos, affiliation revoke and Campus Reps derived from affiliation are added. College logos are cropped and compressed in the admin, and square logos are enforced and re-encoded on the server. (`4969c59`, `853e29a`, `b4973a9`)

### Cover editor and profile

- The Edit cover sheet expands to full screen, with Upload beside Save and larger swatches. (`79e731f`)
- Profile tags share one row when they fit, and the college uses a school emoji. (`144f901`)

### Smaller interface changes

- The Campus communities empty state is minimal. (`856170b`)
- Fields, menus and the notice in Settings are neutral in dark mode. (`c1c296b`)
- The Devices page uses a smartphone icon for app sessions, a status dot for this device and no em dash. (`5a53e9a`)
- Scrollbars are hidden in the Edit cover sheet and the Notifications box. (`8ce9a72`)

## Fixes

- The redundant "College verified" badge is removed from the Campus header. (`576f1b8`)
- The Edit cover sheet opens at its usual height again, and Upload and Save split the footer. (`e833acc`)
- The duplicate Blocked Contacts box is removed from visibility settings. (`c39dfeb`)

## Documentation

- The help articles and the Terms and Privacy text are updated for open signup and Campus verification. (`59d9d71`)
