## Improvements

### Instant Match

- **Direct match requests:** a new socket event lets someone ask one person on the roster to match. It claims both through the same transactional pair claim the ranker uses, with the requester's acceptance recorded up front, so the other side's Accept finishes it normally. Passing, withdrawing or timing out returns both people to searching. Requests are rate limited. (`0c53364`)
- **Redesign:** Instant Match uses Meetifyy's own type, blue and light and dark tokens, with new art drawn in code: a 2.5D campus map with landmarks, pins and routes, a thunderbolt mark and duotone icons instead of emoji. Every screen is rebuilt, from the roster and question steps to the match card, chat frame and the Crew entry card. The roster and searching screens fold people into an avatar summary that slides to a list where anyone can be sent a match request. (`92e3984`)

### Messages, Crew and Campus

- New Message lists your circle with no query, and a search reaches anyone, including unverified people, who are shown with an info notice while the send stays refused on the server. The people pickers also rank by the affinity score the For You feed uses, and in the app the keyboard slides smoothly. (`79b7311`)
- Crew's All tab and college tab now include every activity from your college. For You picked candidates from the soonest-starting activities only, so a college activity outside the soonest 120 was never ranked. A fourth pool brings in the college's upcoming activities. (`2725f54`)
- On phones Crew gets a dedicated activities search page, built like the Communities search. (`e02395a`)
- Create activity picks dates with Start and End dropdowns, with the same scheduling rules as before. (`54f7d2b`) Event length reads like "1 hr 30 min" instead of decimal hours. (`e6d76d2`)
- Campus shortcut pills are taller, the directory shows people as tiles with a round photo, and search headings use sentence case. Verification buttons are taller, and Create Community no longer puts a tick on the selected category. (`ba468d6`, `219052c`)

### Covers and opening screen

- Profile and Community covers share one behaviour: the page's own cover scrolls up and blurs, and pins as the header when the bottom of the avatar or community icon reaches the header's bottom edge. Only transform and opacity change per frame. (`428c705`)
- The opening screen slides advance every 4 seconds and loop, pausing while a finger is down or the app is hidden, and off under reduced motion. Pages can also draw under the side insets. (`736d160`)
- Confessions opens instantly with new intro copy and graphics and a finger-following swipe, because the device remembers whether the account has a name. (`7e1dc70`)

## Fixes

- **Post buttons:** on a phone a tap left Like, Comment, Share and Save stuck in hover, filling the whole pill. Hover styling now applies only with a real pointer, and the press is a smooth shrink. (`d86f8f8`)
- **Media viewer:** dragging down repainted the whole overlay's background every frame. The black is now a separate layer faded by opacity, so the drag is smoother, and the header buttons are grey. (`b0161e7`)
- **Followers list:** its close button did nothing when it was the first page, for example when opened from a notification or a link. (`c26b017`)
- **Profile prompt:** it no longer appears offline, because it now waits for the server and ignores the copy saved on the device. (`514c41a`)
