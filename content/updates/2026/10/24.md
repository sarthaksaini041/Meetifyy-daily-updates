## Improvements

### Instant Match

- **Direct requests:** A new `match:request` socket event lets someone ask one person on the roster to match. It claims both through the same transactional pair claim the ranker uses, with the requester's acceptance recorded up front, so the other side's Accept finishes it normally. Passing, withdrawing or timing out returns both people to searching, and the card describes the target's activity. A request sent before searching starts the search in the same step and undoes it on failure. It is rate limited by a new `im.request.user` policy. (`0c53364`)
- **Redesign:** Instant Match uses Meetifyy's own type, blue and light and dark tokens, with new art drawn in code: a 2.5D campus map with landmarks, pins and routes, a thunderbolt mark, and duotone icons instead of emoji. Every screen is rebuilt: roster, question steps, searching, match card, matched and ended panels, chat frame, launcher and the Crew entry card. The roster and searching screens fold people into an avatar summary that slides to a list where anyone can be sent a match request. (`92e3984`)

### Crew and activities

- **College activities:** For You picked candidates from the soonest-starting activities, follows and interests only, so a college activity outside the soonest 120 was never ranked and the All tab showed none from the viewer's college. A fourth pool now brings in the college's upcoming activities, and the college and campus scopes include "Anyone" activities from the college too. Who may see each one is still decided by the discovery policy in the query. (`2725f54`)
- **Search page:** On phones the Crew page drops its inline search box and + button for a round search icon, opening `/crew/search`. It is built like the Communities search, full-bleed under the system bars, with the filter pills exactly where Crew has them. Desktop keeps the inline search. (`e02395a`)
- **Start and End dropdowns:** The Date & Time modal shows a Start label over day, month and time dropdowns, then the same for End, replacing the calendar grid and time list. The scheduling rules are unchanged, invalid options are disabled, and start dates offer up to a year ahead. (`54f7d2b`)
- **Event length:** Durations read like "1 hr 30 min" or "3 days 3 hr 35 min" instead of decimal hours. (`e6d76d2`)

### Messages and Confessions

- The New Message sheet lists only your circle with no query, ranked by the same learned affinity score as the For You feed, and a search also reaches unverified people with an info notice, while the send stays refused server-side. In the app the keyboard slides over the sheet without re-laying it out. (`79b7311`)
- Confessions open instantly: the device keeps a yes or no per account for having a name, draws the right screen at once and corrects in place. The intro deck has new copy and graphics, and the name step has a "Slip in unseen" button. (`7e1dc70`)

### Covers and opening screen

- **Covers:** Profile and Community share one behaviour. The page's own cover scrolls up and blurs, using a pre-blurred layer faded by opacity, and pins as the header when the bottom of the avatar or community icon reaches the header's bottom edge. Only transform and opacity variables change per scroll event, and the community view remembers images it has shown. (`428c705`)
- **Opening screen:** Slides advance every 4 seconds and loop, with the same spring as a swipe. They pause while a finger is down, stop while the app is hidden and are off under reduced motion. Screens can also draw under a landscape camera cutout or side navigation bar. (`736d160`)

### Smaller UI changes

Campus shortcut pills are taller and the directory shows people as large round tiles three to a row on phones (`ba468d6`). Search labels are sentence case, verification buttons are taller, and Create Community drops the category tick in favour of `aria-pressed` (`219052c`).

## Fixes

- **Profile prompt offline:** The gender and personal email prompt opened from the user's `profileCompletion` without checking where that user came from. The installed app boots from the copy saved on the device, which can be stale, so the prompt appeared offline. It now opens only when online, after auth has loaded and after the server has answered for this session. (`514c41a`)
- **Followers close button:** With no history behind it (opened from a notification, a link or a restored app), `goBack` compared the fallback path to the pathname alone, decided it was already there and did nothing. The comparison includes the query now. (`c26b017`)
- **Media viewer drag:** Dragging repainted the whole overlay's background every frame. The black is a separate layer faded by opacity, the media shrinks slightly as it is pulled, and the header buttons are one dark grey in both themes. (`b0161e7`)
- **Post action buttons:** On a phone a tap left Like, Comment, Share and Save in `:hover`, filling the pill until the next tap elsewhere. Hover applies only with a real pointer, and a held `data-pressed` state is written straight onto the button, since the Android WebView applies `:active` for one frame on a quick tap. (`d86f8f8`)
- **Create-event status bar:** The form fills the screen while marked to be ignored by the sampler, so the status bar icons came from the Campus page behind it and could be white on white. (`e6d76d2`)
