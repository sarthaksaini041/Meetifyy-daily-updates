## Fixes

### Realtime in the installed app

Native sockets were accepted and then disconnected, so Instant Match sat on "Reconnecting". The app now sends its session id and token in the socket handshake (a WebView never stores the session cookie), and they go through the same checks as the cookie. The token is read from the API client at every reconnect, so a reconnect after a token refresh no longer presents the old one. (`3ee873c`, `eaa5f59`)

### Current session on the app

The server couldn't tell which session was the app's own, so "Sign out other devices" and a password change also signed out the device making the request, and the device list showed no current device. It now falls back to the `x-session-id` header when there is no cookie. Two uploads that sent only a bearer token now send it too. (`f5109a1`)

### Share links from the app

Shared post and conversation links from the app pointed at `https://localhost`. They now use the site URL. (`ca7e760`)

### Profile cover polish

- The cover sits under the avatar again, without a shade band, and a pull zooms the page's cover and the header's copy so they line up. (`0061962`)
- The pinned header ends on a clean edge, a pull no longer casts a shadow onto the card (`85d0d61`), and there is no hairline between the two covers in a pull. (`5c2c316`)

### Splash logo

The splash logo is static and the system holds the splash until the app has painted. The breathing animation never ran while the system held the splash, and releasing early to animate it moved the logo. (`f883800`)

## Improvements

### Share sheet, Instagram Stories and App Links

- One share sheet replaces the four share dialogs: a drag-to-dismiss sheet with a multi-select recipient grid and a single Send, plus an external share row with Copy link first.
- Instagram Stories sharing is native and app-only: a story card is drawn per kind (post, profile, community, activity) and handed to Instagram as a sticker. The old web-based path is removed.
- Android App Links open `/post`, `/profile`, `/communities` and `/crew` links in the app.
- Sideways swipes inside a sheet no longer move it.

(`02ad0fb`)

### Faster startup

- On a cold start the app renews its session before the first request instead of after a guaranteed 401, which saves two round trips under the splash. The refresh has an 8-second deadline, and conversations wait for sign-in. (`9224bff`)
- The page tells the app it is ready directly, so the splash lifts about 500 ms sooner, and a returning user's home screen and first feed page load alongside the session restore. (`d8cdd68`)
- framer-motion and socket.io are kept out of the entry chunk, and route preloads wait until after the first screen. Startup JavaScript went from 329.5 kB to 274.4 kB gzipped. (`a2db26f`)

### Smoother profile and pull-to-refresh

- The blurred cover is drawn once into a small canvas instead of two full-size CSS blurs, and scroll effects run on the compositor. The first scroll after opening a profile went from 10 to 13 slow frames to none. (`241e45a`)
- Pull-to-refresh paints the gesture straight onto the page instead of re-rendering React on every touch move. (`387af7c`)
- As you scroll, the avatar shrinks and fades under the header, and the name hands over to the header title frame by frame. (`1b05ca7`)
- Covers are fetched and decoded before the profile opens, and the app cuts to the photo instead of fading in. (`0545483`)

### System bars

The system bars are transparent and painted by the page in the same frame as the change, so they no longer lag a navigation by 70–500 ms. (`d71e8bc`)

### Reply bar and skeletons

- Post replies use a floating reply bar docked above the keyboard, with a Send button once there is text. (`bce746e`)
- Loading skeletons now match the page, keeping headers, tabs and buttons real. Empty states no longer flash while content loads, and several spinners became skeletons. (`ad47162`)
