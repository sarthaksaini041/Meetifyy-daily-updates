## Improvements

### Communities rebuilt

- The Communities page has Your communities and Explore tabs, private join requests and a dedicated community search page. Home gets a Your communities strip, and the old browse grid is removed. (`58c3e97`)
- The backend lists every community the viewer belongs to, with the time of its latest post, and the public list accepts search and visibility filters and flags pending join requests on private communities. (`ff770af`)

### Shared search layout

Search pages share one layout with a discovery sidebar. (`491c386`)

### Mobile app

- Status and navigation bars continue the page beneath them.
- Profile and Community open under a transparent status bar with a collapsing header.
- The splash no longer shifts at launch, and the opening screen hands over to Login without a blank frame.
- Phones stay on the mobile layout in landscape, and pull-to-refresh no longer clips content.
- The new logo is used for the app icon and splash.

(`35beb8f`)

Mobile sheets, modals, navigation and layout also got a round of refinements. (`10c65be`)
