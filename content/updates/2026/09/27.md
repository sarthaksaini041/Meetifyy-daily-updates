## Improvements

### Communities

- `GET /api/communities/mine` returns every community the viewer belongs to, with the time of its latest post. `GET /api/communities` accepts search and visibility filters and flags a pending join request on private communities. (`ff770af`)
- The Communities page has Your communities and Explore tabs, private join requests, a dedicated search page and a Your communities strip on Home. The old browse grid is removed. (`58c3e97`)

### Search

Search gets a shared layout with a discovery sidebar. (`491c386`)

### Mobile app

- Status and navigation bars continue the page beneath them, and Profile and Community open under a transparent status bar with a collapsing header.
- The splash no longer shifts at launch, and the opening screen hands over to Login without a blank frame.
- Phones stay on the mobile layout in landscape, and pull-to-refresh no longer clips content.
- The new logo is used for the app icon and splash.

(`35beb8f`)

### UI refinements

Mobile sheets, modals, navigation and layout get a further round of refinements. (`10c65be`)

## Testing

More backend specs now use typed doubles and `stub<T>()`, with assertions unchanged. Each commit was checked by comparing the assertions before and after.

- **Require-await finish:** The last rewrite for the account-deletion e2e spec, which lives under `test/` and was missed by the earlier commit's staging. (`986af14`)
- **Communities and first-year isolation:** 97 and 94 violations to none. (`a8e1e19`)
- **Auth controller and sanitize-html:** 91 and 91 to none, with deliberately invalid inputs stated where they are used. (`8c4b128`)
- **Deletion lifecycle, OTP and default assets:** Typing the keys map exposed two stale fixture entries for the retired `community-cover` and `profile-cover` assets. The fixtures now register the two real assets. (`0d4fc3a`)
